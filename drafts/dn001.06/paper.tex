\section{Definition}

Let a set defined as $\Omega \in \mathbb{C}$ as the following indices of $\beta \in \mathbb{C}$:

\begin{equation}
	\Omega_{n} = (\beta_{0} \times \beta_{n}) \land \alpha: \Omega_{n} \cup \mathbb{C}
\end{equation}\\
With a variable $\alpha$ with a remainder of $C$ defined as the result of $\beta_{n}$.
